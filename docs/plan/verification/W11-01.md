| Where the sentence went | Result |

**The specified shape was the residue and it does not hold.** A list token is
`\*\*<label>:\*\*[^*]*`, so it runs to the next emphasis marker and the block's last list has none
after it: appended at the end of the block, the sentence is consumed *as part of the
`**Not built:**` list* and never reaches the residue. The scan therefore runs over the block as it
arrived, sentence by sentence, and exempts the **sentence** rather than the block — a sentence
carrying a `**<label>:**` marker has that marker and its backticked names struck out, because those
are the claimed side of a `service-state` comparison that already ran, and whatever prose is left in
it is read like any other. That is what makes row two above red.

`README.md`'s `capability-claims` marker was also moved up to start at **What is not built:**, the
paragraph the original false sentence actually lived in for four milestones. It was outside every
marker until now.
